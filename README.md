### Community Flood Reporting and Response Management System for Sierra Leone

## 1. Project Overview

FloodConnect SL is a proposed digital system designed to improve how communities report flood incidents and how administrators coordinate responses in Sierra Leone.

The system allows residents to submit flood reports, administrators to review and verify reports, and response teams to manage assigned tasks.

## 2. Problem Statement

Flooding affects many communities in Sierra Leone, causing damage to property, disrupting daily activities and creating risks for residents. Reporting incidents and coordinating responses can be challenging when information is not properly organised.

FloodConnect SL proposes a structured way to manage flood reports and response activities.

## 3. Project Objectives

* Provide a platform for residents to report flood incidents.
* Allow administrators to review and verify submitted reports.
* Enable administrators to assign response teams.
* Allow response teams to track and update assigned tasks.
* Improve the organisation of flood-related information.

## 4. Main Users

1. **Residents:** Submit flood reports with their location and incident details.
2. **Administrators:** Review reports, approve or reject them, and assign response teams.
3. **Response Teams:** View assigned tasks and update their progress.

## 5. Main System Modules

* Main System Navigation
* Administrator Login and Report Verification
* Resident Flood Reporting
* Response Team Task Management

## 7. Expected Benefits

* Better organisation of community flood reports.
* Easier review and verification of reported incidents.
* Improved coordination between administrators and response teams.
* Better tracking of assigned response activities.

## 8. Project Scope

FloodConnect SL is an academic project demonstrating the logical design of a community flood reporting and response management system. It is a proposed system and does not currently provide live emergency services, flood prediction or automatic disaster alerts.

## 9. Conclusion

FloodConnect SL demonstrates how information technology can support community-based flood reporting and response coordination in Sierra Leone.
