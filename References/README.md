# REFERENCES

The following sources were consulted to support the research, design and development of the FloodConnect SL project.

1. **National Disaster Management Agency (NDMA), Sierra Leone.** Information on disaster management, flood response and emergency coordination.

2. **United Nations Sierra Leone.** Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs). Information on the global goals, particularly SDG 11 (Sustainable Cities and Communities) and SDG 13 (Climate Action).

3. **International Monetary Fund (IMF).** Sierra Leone: Third Review Under the Extended Credit Facility Arrangement (2026). Information on flood risks and the impact of natural disasters in Sierra Leone.

4. **United Nations Development Programme (UNDP).** Sierra Leone National Adaptation Plan (2021). Information on climate-related risks, seasonal flooding and community resilience.

5. **World Bank.** Freetown, Sierra Leone Flood Risk Assessment. Information and data relating to flood hazards in Freetown.

6. **GitHub Documentation.** Guides on creating repositories, uploading project files, editing README files and managing project versions.

**Note:** These references provide background information and supporting resources. FloodConnect SL is a proposed community flood reporting and response management system, not an operational emergency service.
